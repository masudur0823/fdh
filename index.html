What is FDH

One Digital Hub for Your Entire Distribution Business

FDH (FMCG Distribution Hub) is a centralized digital platform designed for FMCG companies to streamline their entire distribution process.

It connects your field sales, warehouse, accounts, and management into one real-time system — eliminating silos and delivering complete operational clarity.

The Problem

Challenges in Traditional Distribution

📋

Manual Stock Tracking Errors

Paper-based inventory leads to mismatches, ghost stock, and costly reconciliation.

⏱️

Delayed Sales Reports

Reports generated hours later make it impossible to respond to market changes in time.

🔌

Disconnected Accounting Systems

Separate tools create data gaps, double entries, and audit headaches.

🚶

Inefficient Field Sales Monitoring

No visibility into where your reps are or which routes are productive.

💸

Payment & Collection Confusion

Outstanding dues and untracked receivables drain cash flow silently.

📉

Zero Real-Time Visibility

Management relies on end-of-day summaries instead of live dashboards.

FDH solves all these challenges with automation and real-time visibility.
